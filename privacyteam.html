01

Overview

TeamSpinner is a team and name selection tool designed to help users randomly select a participant or divide a list of names into teams. It is part of the Light40X Studio projects by Yassin Diaa.

This Privacy Policy applies to the TeamSpinner website and its features. Please read it before using the service.

02

Information handled by TeamSpinner

TeamSpinner may process information you enter to operate its features, including:

  • Names or labels entered into participant lists.
  • Team names and generated team lists.
  • Theme and display preferences.

TeamSpinner does not require an account in its standard browser-based version. If the website operates entirely in your browser, the names you enter are used to provide the tool's features and are not intentionally sent to a TeamSpinner server.

Do not enter sensitive personal information or other people's information unless you have a valid reason and permission to do so.

03

Local storage

TeamSpinner may use your browser's local storage to remember lists or settings between visits. This data stays in the browser profile on your device unless the website is configured to transmit it elsewhere.

You can remove locally saved information by clearing this website's data in your browser settings. Doing so may reset saved lists and preferences.

04

Third-party services

The website may be hosted by a third-party provider. Hosting providers can process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, request timestamps, and security logs to deliver and protect the website. Their own privacy policies govern their processing.

If TeamSpinner adds analytics, advertising, account services, or cloud synchronization, this policy must be updated to explain the relevant services and the information they receive.

05

Children's privacy

TeamSpinner is a general-purpose utility. Users can enter names into lists, so teachers, parents, and organizers should avoid entering unnecessary personal information about children.

If you have a privacy concern about information submitted through a service connected with TeamSpinner, contact the developer for review.

06

Security and retention

Information stored in browser local storage generally remains there until you clear it, the browser removes it, or the application changes its storage behavior.

No method of online storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device and browser secure, especially when using a shared computer.

07

Your choices

  • Choose what information you enter into TeamSpinner.
  • Remove names and lists through the app where available.
  • Clear saved website data through your browser settings.
  • Stop using the website at any time.
08

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when TeamSpinner's features, technology, or applicable requirements change. The date displayed at the top indicates the latest revision. Please review this page periodically.

09

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or TeamSpinner's handling of information, visit the Light40X portfolio website and use its available contact options.
